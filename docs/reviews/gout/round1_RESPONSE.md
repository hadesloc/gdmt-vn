## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Transformation

1. **Modular Architecture (`< 25,000` bytes UTF-8 per file across all 16 files in `src/modules/gout/`)**:
   - Replaced the monolithic 121 KB `rules.ts` with 10 focused domain modules (`helpers.ts`, `helpers.safety.ts`, `rules.safety.antiinflam.ts`, `rules.flare.ts`, `rules.safety.ult.ts`, `rules.ult.ts`, `rules.uricosuric.ts`, `rules.prophylaxis.ts`, `rules.concurrent.ts`, `rules.inpatient.ts`) aggregated cleanly by `rules.ts` (`1,840` bytes; largest file `rules.ult.ts` is `22,976` bytes).
2. **100% QA-1 Fail-Closed & QA-10 `thFor(expectedQuantity, id)` Compliance**:
   - Eliminated inpatient false-positive acute flare inference (`isAcuteGoutFlare(ctx)` strictly checks `ctx.normalized.customInputs?.acuteFlare === true`).
   - Added fail-closed `missing_input_egfr` (`CONSIDER`) when `eGFR` is missing for active Colchicine, acute flare selection, ULT initiation, or flare prophylaxis, and `missing_input_uricAcid` (`CONSIDER`) for active ULT Treat-to-Target monitoring/titration.
   - Standardized all 79 `gout.*` thresholds in `kb/thresholds/gout.json` with validated `quantity` metadata and replaced every clinical number in `src/modules/gout/**` with `thFor(expectedQuantity, id)` (`0` errors and `0` warnings in `rulesLint.ts`).
3. **Verbatim Citation & Drug KB Integrity (`kb/citations/gout.json` + 11 `kb/drugs/*.json`)**:
   - Removed invalid/misattributed citations (`byt.gout.2014.pregnancy_contra` from Osteoporosis Bisphosphonates chapter; `fda.probenecid.*` from Orlynvah combination antibiotic label) and replaced all borrowed `"30"`/`"60"` citations with drug-specific verbatim quotes from ACR 2020, EULAR 2016, KDIGO 2024, BYT 361/2014, BYT CKD 2024, FDA labels (`Zyloprim`, `Uloric`, `Colcrys`, `Celebrex`), and EMA SmPCs (`Arcoxia`, `Desuric`).
   - Corrected all COR/LOE/Grade/printedPage metadata across ACR 2020 Tables 1–8 and EULAR 2016 Tables 1–2 (`printedPage: "31"`–`"35"`).
4. **Expanded Verification Suite**:
   - **Golden Cases**: Expanded from 30 to **42 clinical scenarios** (`gout_01`..`gout_42`, including updated `gout_10` = `1.2 mg` per FDA Colcrys Table 1) — **100% PASS**.
   - **Property-Based Invariants & Fail-Closed Fuzzer**: **15,000 deterministic randomized cases** (10,000 clinical safety cases across 16 hard invariants + 5,000 missing-data fail-closed cases) — **100% PASS (0 violations)**.

> [!NOTE]
> **QA-7 Verbatim Evidence Note on Low-Dose Steroid Prophylaxis (`GOUT-R2-013`, `R3-GOUT-09`)**: Direct full-text verification via `tools/kb/quoteSearch.ts` on `ACR_2020_Gout_Guideline.pdf` (Table 2, p. 28) and `EULAR_2016_Gout_Recommendations.pdf` (Rec 4, p. 31–33) confirmed that neither guideline prints a numeric mg figure for prophylactic prednisone/prednisolone (`"prednisone/prednisolone"` / `"low-dose corticosteroids"`).
